CoHosts

Internal operators who can open any client account and work the queue — but never this console.
💡 A CoHost is identified by their LinkedIn profile URL, but LinkedIn only tells us the email someone signs in with — the two can't be matched on their own. So adding a CoHost mints a one-time invite link. The first LinkedIn sign-in through that link binds their real account to the record, for good. Nothing to copy, paste or configure at their end.
