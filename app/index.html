What you get

Your LinkedIn inbox is a to-do list pretending to be a chat app.

Two inboxes, hundreds of threads, no idea who is still waiting on you. Pulse turns that mess into a queue that shrinks — and tells you every morning what moved.

🎯

Automatic triage

Every conversation is classified Red, Orange or Green from who spoke last and how long ago. No tagging, no manual upkeep.

📥

Both inboxes, merged

LinkedIn Messaging and Sales Navigator are walked in the same pass and deduplicated into a single list, with the platform kept as a filter.

★

Star and tick

★ marks a conversation you care about; ✓ marks it handled. Both persist across scans, so the follow-up count reflects real progress.

🧩

Chrome extension does the work

One "Sync Now" walks both inboxes in your own logged-in browser, with a live progress bar. Nothing is scraped from the outside.

🔎

Filters that match your pipeline

Slice by follow-up needed, starred, checked, platform, campaign, FIT and INTEREST — or just search a name.

📩

Daily report & health alerts

A morning email recaps what came in and what's still red. If collection ever stops, a watchdog emails you instead of failing silently.

How it works

Three steps to an inbox that manages itself.

1

Sign in with LinkedIn

One click, no password to create or remember. Pulse only ever sees your name and email — LinkedIn confirms who you are and hands back nothing else.

2

Install the collector

Pin the Pulse Chrome extension and hit Sync Now. It walks LinkedIn Messaging and Sales Navigator in your own session and posts the results to your dashboard.

3

Work the red list

Open the dashboard, start at the top of Follow-up needed, and tick your way down. Tomorrow's email tells you what changed overnight.

Security

Invite-only. LinkedIn is the only door.

There is no password form on this site and no public page beyond this one. Access is granted by LinkedIn OpenID Connect and checked against an approved list — if your account isn't on it, the dashboard doesn't exist as far as you're concerned.

Your conversations live in your own Cloudflare KV store. The collector runs inside your browser session, so no credentials are ever shared with a third-party service.

  • No password to share, phish, or leak.
  • Every page and every API route is gated at the edge — not in the browser.
  • Signed, HttpOnly session cookies — revoking access takes effect immediately.
  • Collection happens in your browser — no password sharing with a scraper.
